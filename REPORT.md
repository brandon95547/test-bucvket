## How this was measured

- **Source text** — original.pdf's embedded text layer, extracted losslessly with PyMuPDF.
- **Audio** — transcribed with faster-whisper `medium.en` on cpu/int8, VAD off.
- **Re-worded %** — the share of spoken words NOT inside a run of 8+ consecutive words matching the book in order.
- **Present %** — each book statement is matched against every spoken sentence using sentence-embedding similarity (sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2) plus a check that the numbers and names in it survived. Covered at ≥0.60 similarity, partial at ≥0.42.
