57 Petersham Road   Feb. 23, '40.

Yours of Feb. 18 to hand this A.M.

Re partners. Most important never to divulge your business. They never
understand. But I think all payments to G.M.C. should be credited to your
O.T.O. interests esp. in California where both Smith and Mas S. have
acquired-properties, which will be very useful for your vieuz jours (and mine,
if I can ever get out there!) I want very much to go over and devlop these.
As soon as I can do so, I can put the O.T.O. really on its feet again; for my
presence and personality and literary & dramatic connexions should enable me
to conquer Hollywood. (I can't send photos of the properties because of the
censor. But they look very promising.) ---

Newton has been away ill. I see him Monday, I hope. He has capitalists in
Belgium. So if I can talk him into it, you might be able to explain your
proposition to the man on the spot, and start immediately. ---

I'm in trouble here. My Trust Fund has got cold feet, so that I can't count
on my weekly £2. I had to put your cheque through in consequence. Normally,
though, I'm quite o.k. personally. But in order to push the Tarot I shall have
to renew my wardrobe, at least one suit, amd some underclothes. This will cost
about £30. Then, of course, the printer. I shall probably have to be content
with a one-page war poem for March 21. But please remember that even the
smallest cheque makes all the difference between staying idle and dashing
about to put business through.

P.S. Sorry if this letter seems gloomy. But I have been rather sick, and still
don't feel too gay. I'm worried about the Tarot: we are so near to the end -
and it's the biggest work of my whole life - that I can't help wondering if
some 'tuile' isn't going to drop! I suppose that's liver too.
